## Context

What is the situation? What forces are at play — technical, time, cost? Be honest about the
constraints; they are usually why the decision is not obvious.

## Decision

What you are doing, in one or two sentences.

## Rationale

Why this option over the others. This is the sentence an interviewer will ask you to defend.

## Consequences

- **Easier:** what this unlocks.
- **Harder:** what it costs, what it forecloses.
- **Now required:** what must happen as a result.

## Revisit when

The concrete trigger that should reopen this decision — a metric, a scale threshold, a date. A
decision with no revisit condition is a decision nobody owns.

## What breaks first at 10× / 100× / 1000×

The scale lens. Name the fail point and who solves it at that scale in a real company.
